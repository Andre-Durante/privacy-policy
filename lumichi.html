Lumichi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · Developer: The Anxious XingXing · App: Lumichi (Android, com.lumichi.app)

Lumichi is a virtual pet that helps you learn Mandarin Chinese. It's built to work on your phone, offline. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.

What stays on your phone

All of this is stored only on your device:

  • Your progress: lessons, words, coins, your pet, your house, settings and streaks.
  • Your chats with your pet. Replies are written by an AI model that runs on your phone; your messages are never sent to us or to an AI service.
  • Notes your pet remembers about you, such as your favourite food. You can clear them in Settings.

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also delete the chat history and the optional downloads in Settings.

When the app uses the internet

The app only connects in the cases below, and only after you turn the feature on or tap to start it.

Local weather (optional, off by default). If you turn on weather, the app asks MET Norway's free weather service (api.met.no) for the forecast. It sends rounded coordinates for the city you picked, or your approximate location rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km) if you choose "Use my location". It doesn't send your name or any identifier. MET Norway's own privacy terms apply: https://www.met.no/en/About-us/privacy. If you allow location, the app uses your approximate location once, on your phone, to find the nearest city. It doesn't store or share your precise location.

Optional downloads. The pet's chat brain (about 640 MB), Luma's own voice (about 74 MB) and Luma's ears (about 25 MB) are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) when you tap Download. Like any download, Hugging Face sees your IP address. See their privacy policy at https://huggingface.co/privacy. Nothing about you is uploaded.

Speaking (optional). In "Say it with me" and when you talk to your pet out loud, the app listens after you tap the microphone, to understand what you said. By default it uses "Luma's ears", a small speech-recognition model (about 25 MB, downloaded from Hugging Face when you choose to) that runs entirely on your phone, so your voice never leaves the device. If you choose to use your phone's own speech recognition instead, your phone's speech service, usually Google's, processes the audio, and that service may send it online under its own privacy terms. The app tells you when this applies.

Lumichi never records or keeps your voice.

Reports and emails. If you tap "Report" on a reply or "Send a report" after a problem, your email app opens with a message to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. We use reports only to fix problems and improve the pet's replies.

Backups

Android can back up your progress (not the large downloads) to your own Google account, so it comes back on a new phone. Google handles and encrypts this backup, and we can't see it. You can turn backups off in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Paid features, when available, are bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card details. To confirm a purchase, the app may check it with Google Play. That check uses the purchase token Google provides and no personal details.

Children

Lumichi is a general learning app and isn't directed at children under 13. It has no ads, and you can't chat with other people in it. The on-device chat is limited to simple language-learning conversations.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
