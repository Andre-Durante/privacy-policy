TalentPing Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

What TalentPing Does

TalentPing is a browser extension that helps recruiters track candidates, interview processes, and follow-up reminders.

Data We Collect

Account Information (via Google Sign-In)

When you sign in with Google, TalentPing receives your email address and an authentication token via Supabase Auth. This is used solely to verify that your account has been granted access to the extension. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, or any other Google account data.

Candidate Data

All candidate records, notes, tags, and reminders you create are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local) and are never transmitted to or stored on any server. This data stays on your device and is included only if you manually export it.

LinkedIn Data

TalentPing reads the URL of the active browser tab to detect LinkedIn profile pages and pre-fill a candidate's name and profile link. TalentPing does not scrape, read, or modify the content of any web page.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Your email address is stored in our access-control database (Supabase) solely to determine whether you are authorized to use the extension.

Data Retention

You can delete your locally stored data at any time by removing the extension or using the in-app data management tools. To request removal of your account/email from our access list, contact us at the email below.
