RotaFC Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

What RotaFC Is

RotaFC is a browser-based football prediction and team-building game available at rotafc.com. Users build a fictional club, rent real football players into a lineup for simulated matches, and predict real match outcomes to earn in-game currency.

Data We Collect

Account Information

Sign-up and sign-in are handled by our authentication provider, Clerk. Clerk processes your email address and manages your session; RotaFC stores a reference to your Clerk account alongside your in-game data (see below). See Clerk's own privacy policy for how Clerk itself handles your account data.

Gameplay Data

We store the game state tied to your account: your club (name, crest, colors), coin balance, lineups and formations you've saved, match predictions, and match results involving your club. This data exists to make the game work and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Cookies and Advertising

RotaFC uses cookies required for authentication (via Clerk) to keep you signed in. RotaFC also displays advertising via Google AdSense, which may set cookies or use similar technologies to serve ads, including personalized ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings or via aboutads.info.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers that make RotaFC work: Clerk (authentication), Neon (database hosting), and Google AdSense (advertising). Real football data (fixtures, results, player stats) is sourced from third-party sports-data providers and is not personal data about you.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your account and gameplay data are retained while your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

RotaFC is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If RotaFC's data-handling practices change, this privacy policy will be updated to reflect that before the change takes effect.
