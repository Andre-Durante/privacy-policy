Pocki Privacy Policy and Terms

Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Developer: Anxious XingXing

What Pocki Is

Pocki is a free expense tracking app for Android, developed by Anxious XingXing, that helps individuals and couples track their everyday spending. You can keep several bank accounts, cards and cash in different currencies, organise spending with your own categories, and share an account (such as a joint account) with a partner so you both see and add its transactions. Pocki can also read payment notifications from your banking apps and suggest expenses for you to accept, edit or decline.

Pocki has no subscriptions, no paid features and no ads. Users can optionally leave a small voluntary tip ("Buy me a coffee") to support its development.

Data We Collect

Your Account

Your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash). If you sign in with Google, Pocki receives your email address and the basic profile details Google shares, such as your name and profile picture.

Your Profile (Optional)

You can add a nickname, a colour and a profile photo. They are shown only to you and to the people you share an account with, next to the transactions you add. Photos are stored in private storage and are deleted when you remove them or delete your account.

What You Enter

Accounts, balances, transactions, categories, descriptions and notes, and settings such as your display currency.

Sharing

When you share an account, Pocki stores the email address you invite. People you share an account with can see that account, its transactions, the categories used on it, and your email address. Nothing is shared until they accept your invite.

Notifications (Auto-Capture, Android)

Auto-capture is optional and stays off until you turn it on and grant Android's "notification access". Pocki then reads notifications only from the apps you choose (for example, your banking app), on your phone. Payment notifications are kept on your phone as suggestions. Nothing is sent to Pocki's servers unless you accept a suggestion, and then only the resulting transaction (amount, description, date and account) is saved, together with a technical identifier that prevents duplicates. The notification text itself is not uploaded. For apps you haven't chosen, Pocki only remembers the app's name if it sent a payment-like notification, so it can suggest it in settings. You can turn auto-capture off at any time in Android settings.

Optional Tips

If you choose to leave a tip, the payment is made through Google Play. Pocki only receives an order record (product, amount, country and date); it never sees your name, email or payment details.

How We Use Your Data

Only to run Pocki: to sign you in, store and sync your records across your devices and with the people you share accounts with, and to answer your messages. Pocki does not show ads, does not sell your data, and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

Data Sharing and Storage

Pocki relies on these service providers:

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in, in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Google, if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Google Play processes optional tips.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access rules in the database ensure each person can only see their own data and the accounts that were shared with them. Pocki's data is excluded from Android device backups.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is kept until you delete your account, which you can do at any time in the app under Settings → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, your private accounts and their transactions, your categories and your invites. Accounts you shared with someone are handed over to them, so their records stay intact. Transactions you added to someone else's shared account stay in their records but are no longer linked to you.

Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your Pocki account and all its data at any time:

  • In the app: open Settings → Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Without the app: email gearcorphub@gmail.com from the email address you use for Pocki, with the subject "Delete my Pocki account". Your account will be deleted within 30 days and you will get a confirmation email.

What is deleted: your account, your profile and photo, your private accounts and their transactions, your categories, recurring payments and invites. What is kept: accounts you shared with someone are handed over to them so their records stay intact, and transactions you added to someone else's shared account stay in their records without any link to you. Nothing else is retained.

Your Rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to how it is used, by emailing the address below. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children's Privacy

Pocki is not intended for children under 16.

Terms of Use

Pocki is provided free of charge and "as is". It is a tool to help you keep track of your money; it does not give financial advice, and you are responsible for checking that your records are accurate. Suggestions created from notifications may be wrong, which is why Pocki never saves them without your approval. Please don't use Pocki to store information you are not allowed to share, and only invite people you trust to your accounts.

Tips and Refunds

Tips are voluntary, one-time payments that support Pocki's development. They don't unlock any features, and no goods are delivered. If you tipped by mistake, request a refund through Google Play, or email us within 30 days and we will refund it.

Changes to This Policy

If Pocki's data-handling practices change, this page will be updated before the change takes effect, and important changes will be announced in the app.
